## Description

The goal of the project was to propose a novel HVAC system capable of taking
temperature data from multiple rooms and redirecting heat flow from one room to
another for more efficient heat management. The system also considers outdoor
weather to decide when to turn on the heater, open a window, or turn on the AC.

> Start date: January 2020 · Status: Completed
